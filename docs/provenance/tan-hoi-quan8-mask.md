# Tân Hội — QUAN-8 and GCOV mask correction

This candidate export uses CONTRACT-001 2.0.0, 2,344 Tân Hội cells and 22 NISAR acquisitions from 11 August through 3 November 2026. It adds the 30 October 083A and 3 November 148D acquisitions from QUAN-8; the CMR catalog is an interim snapshot, not the final H7 catalog. The 75% pixel classification and asymmetric 50% filter remain post-hoc selections after DSWx-S1 comparison, not independent validation or field accuracy. [Earlier 20-image v2 snapshot](tan-hoi-v2.md).

S1 now treats GCOV mask 0 and 255 as unavailable in the main image and reference windows; valid masks are 1–254. The original 20-image v2 reader did not apply this mask. A local replay of those 20 images found 19 changed raw cell-observations and 20 changed filtered cell-observations on 11 cells. Status and `flag_gt30` did not change in that replay, but some onset/duration fields did. The two new images and the mask correction are both included in this candidate; their combined change must not be attributed to either one alone.

The 22-image cube SHA-256 is `b8fb8fd0f65314661975957d2c2deb909673700756c0ec4d109b2de6cc073eae`; the matching S5 metrics SHA-256 is `1ac2466c51c0b81c1654ae31bb1638117c3b3506e57ed1ddf6c5ed4c74166c43`. S6 and S7 received the same cube and metrics. Existing local raster caches were copied, paired by acquisition and cache identity with their original GCOV masks, and transformed into new mask-aware caches; the 20 frozen source cache hashes were checked against the prior snapshot. Source cache and mask files are not public outputs. The BANG-2 landcover CSV SHA-256 is `47106610fda1f5b677e53eeb2788baf41bd4cce6efc1500aff508d422902e36f`.

S6 reads the exact `valid` and `water` labels from Bằng's 26-pass per-cell DSWx table (CSV SHA-256 `3ef47d18441459f49f634e484c413b6362058287ee21321ea366ab33d565fab9`). Its converted NPZ SHA-256 is `5ad6ba9f2e5924fabc977685cd1a419bf201b85fd1f642e9ea94db7de0face11`. Replaying the earlier 20-image cube with these labels reproduced the existing S6 filtered and unfiltered results, pair records, and filtered-DSWx side comparison exactly. The table does not contain exact class-1-plus-class-3 labels; rounded shares cannot reconstruct nine boundary cases with certainty, so that optional comparison is omitted rather than invented.

S6 pairs 19 NISAR acquisitions to DSWx observations within three days. For the filtered `flag_gt30` comparison, the table has 1,394 cells where both methods produce yes/possible/no: precision is 369/980 = 0.3765 and recall is 369/413 = 0.8935. The filtered result reports 1,295 NISAR yes cells, or 8,093.75 ha at 6.25 ha per cell; this is not a methane measurement. Different comparison subsets and possible/NA treatment mean these numbers must not be directly compared to a result with a different denominator.

`validation.json.frozen_utc` preserves the original H3 baseline freeze, not a pre-selection freeze of the updated pixel method. PH-01 preserves the historic method change; PH-02 records the additional acquisitions and mask correction. `meta.method.frozen_utc` is null because this candidate has not been independently frozen/accepted. Reference agreement is post-hoc throughout.

Bang Rakam 48×48 is separate. Its 20 m/10 m mixed-resolution processing and six 112A scenes with no valid mask samples are not included in this export; its exploratory results have not been published.
