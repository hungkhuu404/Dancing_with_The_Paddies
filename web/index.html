Water observations

Each field uses the 250 m block at its centre. Observation dates bound a transition; they do not give its exact time. Radar does not measure water depth.

Scene and imagery

On date changes, an illustrated sweep briefly emphasizes fields observed with water. This emphasis does not represent radar brightness.

The radar view uses a fixed NISAR HH reference from 27 Oct 2026. Object responses are illustrative. Imagery is from EOxCloudless 2016 and contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (CC BY 4.0). Map features are credited to © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Scene ecology, architecture, traffic and irrigation are illustrative. They do not measure depth or gate operations. Asset provenance and OSM infrastructure provide the source details. Atlas geography: Natural Earth (public domain). Weather: Open-Meteo / ERA5 (CC BY 4.0); optical metadata: Sentinel-2 via Earth Search. Astronomy: SunCalc, Vladimir Agafonkin (BSD-2-Clause). Stars and radar ray paths are illustrative.

Selected acquisition weather

Audio

Data sound uses each NISAR observation: more tones mean a greater water share among observed blocks; high and low accents mark newly wet and newly dry blocks. Two low ticks mean no observation, one means less than half coverage. It does not represent water depth.

Tour narration: Jon, synthesised with ElevenLabs. Tour music is an original procedural composition. Ambient sound is an original procedural illustration, not a field recording. Voice asset details.
