Reading the water record

Each block covers 250 m by 250 m. Radar observations show open water, not water depth.

Two ways to read the map

Season shows the record across all available observations. Choosing a date shows the latest valid water state on or before that date. A missing image does not mean the field was dry.

Date ranges, not exact days

Water can arrive or recede between satellite visits. The record keeps both date bounds. Radar can date the start early on dry, flat fields. Pale status colours indicate a single, unconfirmed observation.
